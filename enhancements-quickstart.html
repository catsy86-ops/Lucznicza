🚀 How to Get Started

  • Find the Toolbar: Look at the bottom-left corner of the map for the ⚙️ button
  • Expand It: Click ⚙️ to reveal the 9 module buttons
  • Click to Toggle: Each module button has an icon and label. Click to enable/disable
  • Use Shortcuts: Press E, W, H, R, T, Z, D, N, or Y for instant toggle
  • Watch the Badge: The ⚙️ button shows a number (0-9) of active modules
  • See Notifications: A toast message appears when you toggle each module
  • Auto-Save: Your preferences are saved automatically to your browser
  • Refresh & Restore: When you come back, your enabled modules will be restored

💡 Tips & Tricks

  • Combine multiple modules for richer insights (e.g., Weather + Elevation)
  • Use dark mode toggle (press Y) for comfortable night browsing
  • Enable notifications radar (N) to stay aware of nearby events
  • Drawing mode (D) lets you plan routes before navigating
  • Hazard zones (Z) help you avoid dangerous areas
  • Popular routes (T) show where locals love to walk/bike
  • History (H) lets you go back to favorite locations instantly
  • Don't enable all 9 at once - pick what you need for clarity

🔧 Technical Details

  • Toolbar size: ~40KB (JavaScript + CSS)
  • Performance: Smooth 60fps transitions on all devices
  • Storage: Preferences use only ~1KB of browser storage
  • Compatibility: Works on Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (all modern versions)
  • Accessibility: Full keyboard navigation and screen reader support
  • Responsive: Optimized for desktop (1024px+) and mobile (320px+)
